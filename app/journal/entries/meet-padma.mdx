Padma is my personal assistant. She's on a US business WhatsApp number, so you can message her the same way you'd message anyone else.

She's built on Kapso's free plan. Kapso is a WhatsApp API platform, which is how she actually lives in WhatsApp instead of some separate app.

She's connected to my shop website, to my Twitter/X, and to my AI agent, Grok Bot, which ties things together. If you want to say hi, she's around.
